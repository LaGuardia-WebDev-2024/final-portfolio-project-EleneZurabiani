Operetta Workshop

Welcome the THE OPERAAA! This year we worked on the production of Gershwins, Of The I Sing! The rehearsals were super tiring, especially during tech week we were all collectivley losing it...
But the final outcome was so amazing and I'm very happy to have had the chance to participate in this show with so many amazing people.
I'll always miss the memories I made in this class like getting yelled at for not knowing the choreo, combined rehearsals with the orchestra, goofing off when we needed to lock in, "chipotle", "chick-fil-a", and "McDonalds",
and of course... The opera party...
